Tool guides and workflows for viral informatics.

The iVirus Pipeline

Follow these eight steps to go from raw sequencing reads to annotated and classified viral populations. Click any tool name to jump to its full guide.

Step Goal Key tools
1 · Raw Reads Start with FASTQ files from your sequencer or the SRA —
2 · QC & Trim Assess read quality; remove adapters and low-quality bases FastQC, BBDuk, fastp, MultiQC, Trimmomatic
3 · Assembly Assemble short reads into contigs MetaSPAdes, MEGAHIT, SPAdes
4 · Viral ID Detect viral contigs; assess genome quality and completeness VirSorter2, VIBRANT, VirSorter, CheckV
5 · Gene Calling Predict protein-coding genes from viral contigs Prodigal, Prokka
6 · Annotation Annotate gene functions; identify auxiliary metabolic genes DRAM-v, VIGA, Cenote-Taker 2
7 · Taxonomy Classify viral genomes into genus-level groups vConTACT2, VIRIDIC, CAT/BAT
8 · Ecology & Viz Predict hosts; estimate abundance; explore community structure iPHoP, WIsH, Anvi'o, Cytoscape
