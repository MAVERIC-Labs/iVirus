iVirus Citations

The main publications describing iVirus. Please cite these if you decide to use these resources!

2021

  1. iVirus 2.0: Cyberinfrastructure-supported tools and data to power DNA virus ecology — Bolduc, B., et al.
    ISME Communications (2021) DOI: 10.1038/s43705-021-00083-3 iVirus
    An update to the original iVirus work. iVirus expands to KBase, adding new tools, datasets, and workflows alongside continued CyVerse support.

2017

  1. iVirus: facilitating new insights in viral ecology with software and community data sets imbedded in a cyberinfrastructure — Bolduc, B., Youens-Clark, K., Roux, S., Hurwitz, B. L., Sullivan, M. B.
    ISME J (2017) DOI: 10.1038/ismej.2016.89 iVirus
    The original iVirus paper, introducing the project and its community data and software resources within CyVerse.

Publications using iVirus

Research that used iVirus tools, datasets, or workflows. Authors: please add your work by contacting us.

2020

  1. Viral Ecogenomics of Arctic Cryopeg Brine and Sea Ice — Zhong, Z.-P., Rapp, J. Z., Wainaina, J. M., Solonenko, N. E., Maughan, H., Carpenter, S. D., Sullivan, M. B.
    mSystems (2020) DOI: 10.1128/mSystems.00246-20 uses iVirus
    Viral ecogenomics of extreme Arctic environments using iVirus-based analytical workflows.

2019

  1. Marine DNA Viral Macro- and Microdiversity from Pole to Pole — Gregory, A. C., Zayed, A. A., Conceição-Neto, N., Temperton, B., Bolduc, B., Alberti, A., Wincker, P., et al.
    Cell (2019) DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2019.03.040 uses iVirus
    Global-scale characterization of marine DNA viral diversity from pole to pole, leveraging iVirus tools for single-amplified virus analysis.
  2. Long-read viral metagenomics captures abundant and microdiverse viral populations and their niche-defining genomic islands — Warwick-Dugdale, J., Solonenko, N., Moore, K., Chittick, L., Gregory, A. C., Allen, M. J., Temperton, B., et al.
    PeerJ (2019) DOI: 10.7717/peerj.6800 uses iVirus
    Demonstrates iVirus tools applied to long-read viromics, revealing abundant and microdiverse viral populations and their genomic islands.
  3. Taxonomic assignment of uncultivated prokaryotic virus genomes is enabled by gene-sharing networks — Bin Jang, H., Bolduc, B., Zablocki, O., Kuhn, J. H., Roux, S., Adriaenssens, E. M., Sullivan, M. B.
    Nature Biotechnology (2019) DOI: 10.1038/s41587-019-0100-8 uses iVirus
    Introduces the vConTACT2 framework — the updated gene-sharing network approach for assigning virus taxonomy.

2018

  1. Soil Viruses Are Underexplored Players in Ecosystem Carbon Processing — Trubl, G., Jang, H. B., Roux, S., Emerson, J. B., Solonenko, N., Vik, D. R., Rich, V. I.
    mSystems (2018) DOI: 10.1128/mSystems.00076-18 uses iVirus
    Applied iVirus tools to explore viruses in soil ecosystems and their underappreciated roles in carbon cycling.

  1. Single-virus genomics reveals hidden cosmopolitan and abundant viruses — Martinez-Hernandez, F., Fornas, O., Lluesma Gomez, M., Bolduc, B., de la Cruz Peña, M. J., Martínez, J. M., Martinez-Garcia, M.
    Nature Communications (2017) DOI: 10.1038/ncomms15892 uses iVirus

  2. Putative archaeal viruses from the mesopelagic ocean — Vik, D. R., Roux, S., Brum, J. R., Bolduc, B., Emerson, J. B., Padilla, C. C., Sullivan, M. B.
    PeerJ (2017) DOI: 10.7717/peerj.3428 uses iVirus
    Discovery of putative archaeal viruses from the mesopelagic ocean, identified using iVirus tools integrated into CyVerse.
  3. vConTACT: an iVirus tool to classify double-stranded DNA viruses that infect Archaea and Bacteria — Bolduc, B., Jang, H. B., Doulcier, G., You, Z.-Q., Roux, S., Sullivan, M. B.
